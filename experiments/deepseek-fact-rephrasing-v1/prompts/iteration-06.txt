You receive exact dialogue quotations selected in a coverage-first sweep. Return
ONLY JSON: {"facts":[{"text":"...","quote":"..."}]}.

For every defensible quote, copy `quote` exactly and produce one clause-level,
standalone claim supported by that quote alone. Retain the supplied speaker label
whenever the statement is a belief, proposal, prediction, report, or viewpoint.
Keep every hedge/modal/negation/condition/exception/quantity/time marker, either
verbatim or with an equally weak semantic form. Never use an ellipsis.

Before outputting a card, reject it if (a) a pronoun such as it/this/that/we/them
lacks an antecedent in the fact text, (b) it contains more than one independent
predicate, (c) it combines a mechanism/reason with an outcome, or (d) any word
is not supported by the one quote. It is better to omit a malformed candidate
than repair it with context from another quote. Emit no duplicates.
